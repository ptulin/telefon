# Product Spec

## Product Statement

Personal AI Phone is a simplified AI assistant app designed for people who do not want to manage a complex smartphone app grid.

It should feel like talking to a helpful, trusted person who can call someone, check the calendar, remember important life details, understand photos and documents, and gradually take over more tasks.

## Primary Users

- older adults
- non-technical users
- busy users who want fewer app decisions
- users interested in transitioning away from traditional smartphone behavior

## UX Principles

- one main surface
- large controls
- clear confirmations
- minimal jargon
- memory of people, routines, and preferences
- no forced app-switching
- voice-first but touch-complete

## MVP Jobs To Be Done

- call a person by name or relationship
- check what is happening today
- add a calendar event with natural language
- remind me to do something later
- read and explain a document or letter
- look at a photo and explain what it shows
- remember personal facts and preferences

## Trust Model

Low-risk actions:

- read contacts
- read calendar
- answer questions
- summarize documents

Medium-risk actions:

- create reminders
- create calendar events
- send drafts
- prepare purchases

High-risk actions:

- spend money
- send sensitive data
- book or confirm important appointments
- share personal records

High-risk actions require clear human confirmation.

## Future Product Direction

- AI-to-AI scheduling and coordination
- merchant and service agents
- recurring personal task automation
- gradually replacing messaging, shopping, and service-navigation apps
